Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Heavy Hitter Transport LLC ("Heavy Hitter," "we," "us") is a New York box truck transportation company. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our online quote builder, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Quote and booking details: your name, phone number, email address, pickup and drop-off addresses, job date and time, the service you choose, add-ons, and any notes you enter.
  • Partner and business forms: the information you submit when you contact us as a business account or equipment partner.
  • Calls, texts and emails: what you send us when you contact us directly.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting providers may log technical information such as IP address and browser type.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes, confirm and schedule jobs, and contact you about your job.
  • To respond to questions, partner and business inquiries.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to do the job (for example, with the driver or crew handling your move or delivery), with service providers that host or operate this website and our booking tools, or when required by law.

Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your quote or job. Reply STOP to a text to opt out of further texts.

Retention and security

We keep job records as long as needed to run the business and meet legal and tax obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, subject to records we must keep, by contacting us below.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
